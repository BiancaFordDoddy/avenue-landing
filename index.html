Everything in one place

Fewer group texts. More peace of mind.

Who’s taking Mom?

Every appointment shows who’s driving, and the doctor’s number is one tap away.

Bills, never late.

See what’s due this week, mark it paid, and repeating bills add themselves.

Snap a bill.

Take a photo and AVENUE fills it in. You check it, then save.

Every number, finally in one place.

Doctors, pharmacies, aides, insurance and utilities. You choose who sees each card.

Notes for the doctor.

Flag what matters (“dizzy when standing, twice this week”) and it’s waiting at the next appointment.

Built for the whole care circle.

Invite siblings, a spouse or a paid aide. Each person sees what they need. Care helpers never see bills, and you decide who sees each contact.

Their own account

Built around Mom, not just for her.

The person you care for can have their own AVENUE, with large text and just today and tomorrow. They can mark medicines taken, write a note, or call you with one tap.

IndependentThe full app, like any family member.
SimplifiedA calmer view. Nothing can be deleted or moved by mistake.
PausedA kind message and a button to call family.

They’re always told when their view changes, and they can choose ahead of time who may change it.

Private by design

Your family’s business stays yours.

Nothing we don’t need.

AVENUE never stores passwords, full account numbers or Social Security numbers. Bill photos are deleted as soon as they’re read.

You decide who sees what.

Bills, contacts and each person’s access are set by you, and enforced by the database itself, not just the screen.

Every change is recorded.

Medication changes and who made them are kept, so the family and the doctor always have the full story.

Caring for someone you love is a full-time job. Keeping track of it shouldn’t be.

Today

The whole day, at a glance.

Open AVENUE and see what’s next: the next dose, who’s driving to physical therapy, and what the family noticed today.

  • A gentle arc of the day, morning to night
  • A morning brief and evening-before reminders

Medications

Nobody has to ask, “Did she take it?”

Every dose has a time and a person. Mark it taken and the whole family sees it, along with every change the doctor makes.

Appointments

Who’s taking Mom?

Each visit shows who’s driving, where to go, and the questions the family flagged for the doctor. The office is one tap away.

Bills

Never late again.

See what’s due this week and mark it paid. Repeating bills add themselves. Snap a photo of a paper bill and AVENUE fills it in for you to check.

Contacts

Every number, finally in one place.

Doctors, the pharmacy, aides, insurance and the electric company. You choose who sees each card, and passwords are never stored.

Care notes

Everyone sees how she’s doing.

A few words a day from whoever was there. Flag anything for the doctor and it’s waiting at the next appointment.

Calm by day.
Gentle at night.

AVENUE follows the sun. When you check on things at two in the morning, it’s kind to tired eyes.

Their own account

Built around Mom, not just for her.

The person you care for can have their own AVENUE: large text, just today and tomorrow, and one button to call you. You choose how much they can do, and they’re always told when it changes.

IndependentThe full app, like anyone in the family.
SimplifiedA calmer view. Nothing can be deleted or moved by mistake.
PausedA kind message and a button to call family.

They can choose ahead of time who may change it. Dignity first, for as long as possible.

Private by design

Your family’s business stays yours.

Nothing we don’t need.

AVENUE never stores passwords, full account numbers or Social Security numbers. Bill photos are deleted as soon as they’re read.

You decide who sees what.

Bills, contacts and each person’s access are set by you, and enforced by the database itself, not just the screen.

The full story, kept.

Every medication change is recorded with who made it and why, so the family and the doctor are never guessing.
